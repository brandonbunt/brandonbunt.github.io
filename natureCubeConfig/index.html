natureCube

A handmade e-ink object that replaces your phone for everything that doesn't need a phone.

Your phone tells you the weather. So does natureCube — but it doesn't also show you seventeen notifications, a news feed, and an ad.

natureCube is an always-on e-ink display for your home. It shows the time, the season, the sky tonight, the traditional Japanese calendar, the day's character. Then it stops. No backlight. No alerts. No engagement loop.

This is a live prototype of the configuration interface. Build your own day arc and see what it looks like on the display.
